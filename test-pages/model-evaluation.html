💡 What Do These Metrics Mean?

  • Accuracy: Percentage of overall predictions that were completely correct across both legitimate and phishing classes.
  • Precision: Out of all websites the model predicted as phishing, how many were actually phishing (measures low false alarm rate).
  • Recall: Out of all actual phishing websites in the test set, how many the model correctly detected (measures coverage).
  • F1-Score: The harmonic mean balancing Precision and Recall into a single representative score.

These figures are the model's score on its held-out test data, so they stay the same until the model is retrained. The AI analysis of a specific website is in the extension popup (AI model result) and in each scan's details on the History page.

⚠️ Model Limitations & Disclaimer: Evaluation metrics are calculated on a held-out split grouped by domain. The Random Forest model evaluates numerical URL and structural DOM features. A high accuracy score indicates statistical model quality but does not constitute an absolute guarantee of safety for unanalyzed real-world pages.
